## How the AGPL and this policy fit together

- **The code** is licensed under the AGPL v3 only. You may run, study, change and share it
  under that license.
- **The AGPL grants no trademark rights**, and under section 7(e) we decline to grant them. See
  [ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md).
- **The brand assets are not licensed under the AGPL.** We keep all rights in them, except the
  permissions this policy gives.
- **The attribution and legal notices are license conditions.** Under section 7(b) of the AGPL
  and our additional terms, every copy and every modified version must keep the "Powered by
  KNOQRA" attribution and the legal notices visible in its user interface. Removing them breaks
  the license terms, and section 8 of the AGPL says what happens then.

## What you may do without asking

1. **Self-host KNOQRA, as is or modified, under the KNOQRA name**, for your own organization,
   with the KNOQRA branding, the "Powered by KNOQRA" attribution and the legal notices left
   visible and unchanged. Self-hosting needs a free self-host license key (see the
   [README](README.md#license-brand-and-white-label)).
2. **Keep the attribution.** Displaying "Powered by KNOQRA" with a link to
   <https://knoqra.app>, as the additional terms require, is always allowed.
3. **Refer to KNOQRA truthfully.** You may say that your integration "works with KNOQRA", that
   you offer "KNOQRA setup and support", or that a product is "built on KNOQRA", as long as you
   use the plain word KNOQRA, don't use the logo or Otto, and don't suggest that we made,
   endorse or sponsor your product or service.
4. **Write about KNOQRA**: articles, reviews, tutorials, talks and videos, including screenshots
   of the unmodified product.
5. **Contribute** to the KNOQRA repository. The [CLA](CLA.md) covers your contributions.

## What needs a commercial license

Without a written license from us, you may not:

1. **Rebrand KNOQRA.** Present KNOQRA, or a modified version of it, under another name, logo or
   brand, to your own users or to anyone else. This includes reselling or hosting it for clients
   under your brand ("white-labeling").
2. **Remove or hide the branding.** Remove, replace, rename or obscure the KNOQRA name, logo,
   Otto, the "Powered by KNOQRA" attribution or the legal notices. Obscuring includes making
   them invisible, too small to read, the same color as the background, hidden from assistive
   technology, or switched off by a setting or flag.
3. **Use the KNOQRA Marks in your own names**: a product, company, service, domain, app-store
   listing or social media account name that contains KNOQRA or Otto, or something confusingly
   similar.
4. **Run a hosted or managed service for others under the KNOQRA name**, or present any service
   as the official KNOQRA Cloud.
5. **Suggest endorsement or partnership** that we have not agreed to in writing.
6. **Use the KNOQRA name on a build that removes or bypasses the self-host license-key check.**
   The AGPL lets you change the code. It does not let you use our brand on a version we have not
   approved. Such a build gets no permission to use the KNOQRA Marks, apart from the attribution
   and notices the additional terms require.
7. **Change the brand assets** (recolor, redraw or animate the logo or Otto) except to resize
   them, or use them on merchandise.

## Commercial white-label license

The commercial white-label license is for organizations that want to offer KNOQRA under their
own brand. It is a separate written agreement with us. Depending on the agreement, it grants:

- the right to rebrand the product with your own name, logo and colors;
- a release from the attribution and legal-notice terms in
  [ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md);
- a commercial copyright license to the code, in place of the AGPL, so you need not publish
  your modifications. The [CLA](CLA.md) lets us grant this for code contributed by others too.

## Infringement

Using the KNOQRA Marks outside this policy infringes our trademark rights. Removing or hiding the
attribution or the legal notices, or distributing the brand assets outside this policy, also
infringes our copyright and breaks the AGPL terms. If that happens we may ask you to stop, and we
may ask for the commercial license fee that would have applied, backdated to when the use began.
We may also seek the remedies the law provides, such as a court order to stop the use, and
damages.

We would rather license you than take action. If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask
first.
