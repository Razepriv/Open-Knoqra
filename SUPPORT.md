# Support

## Questions and help

- **GitHub Discussions:** use the repository's Discussions once they are enabled.
- **Issues:** until then, use the [question form](https://github.com/Razepriv/Open-Knoqra/issues/new?template=question.yml), which applies the `question` label.

Before asking, check the [README](README.md), the [development guide](docs/development.md) and the [architecture notes](docs/architecture.md). The troubleshooting section of the development guide covers the most common local setup problems.

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the issue forms: [open an issue](https://github.com/Razepriv/Open-Knoqra/issues/new/choose). Search existing issues first.

## Security problems

Do not use issues or discussions. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Conduct

Everyone taking part is expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
