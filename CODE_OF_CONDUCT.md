# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want KNOQRA to be a project where anyone can take part without being harassed. We pledge to make participation a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that build an open, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a home address or phone number, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reasons for a moderation decision when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public, for example when using an official project account or speaking at an event on its behalf.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **conduct@knoqra.dev** (placeholder address, to be confirmed before the repository goes public). All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in order, when deciding the consequences of a violation.

1. **Correction.** For use of inappropriate language or other behavior judged unprofessional or unwelcome. Consequence: a private, written message explaining the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a violation through a single incident or a series of actions. Consequence: a warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this Code of Conduct, for a set period. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior. Consequence: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a set period. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violating community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of a person, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people. Consequence: a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
